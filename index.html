Overlapping windows can’t share money. In UPI/ASBA IPOs each applicant’s funds stay blocked until ~2 working days after the close, so IPOs open on the same days need their cash at the same time — per PAN. The counter above sums every contribution you have recorded: treat it as the peak the group must keep free, not a recyclable pot.
