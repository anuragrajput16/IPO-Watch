Overlapping windows can’t share money. In UPI/ASBA IPOs your funds stay blocked until ~2 working days after each close, so IPOs open on the same days need their cash at the same time. The counter above sums everything you select — treat it as the peak you must keep free, not a recyclable pot.

Live GMP: figures are pulled from a gmp.json file that a scheduled job refreshes every few hours, sourced from ipoji.com. Press Refresh GMP to re-read the latest. Open this page from your GitHub Pages URL (not the local file) for live data.

GMP is unofficial grey-market data — a rough sentiment signal, not a forecast, and sources disagree. Application amounts are 1 lot at the upper band (cut-off). None of this is financial advice.
